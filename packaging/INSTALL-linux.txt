YeOldeVST @VERSION@ - Linux x86_64

Hosts native Linux VST 1.x/2.x plugins (.so files) in any DAW that loads CLAP or VST3.
(Windows .dll plugins need the Windows version of YeOldeVST.)

Install (pick the format your DAW uses, or both):

  CLAP: copy YeOldeVST.clap to
        ~/.clap                  (just you)
     or /usr/lib/clap            (all users)

  VST3: copy the whole YeOldeVST.vst3 folder to
        ~/.vst3                  (just you)
     or /usr/lib/vst3            (all users)

Then rescan plugins in your DAW. Add "YeOldeVST Instrument", "YeOldeVST Instrument (multi-out)"
or "YeOldeVST Effect" to a track, open its window, add the folders with your VST2 .so plugins
(often ~/.vst or /usr/lib/vst) and press Scan. Double-click a plugin to load it.

Needs: glibc 2.35 or newer (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and later), X11 or XWayland,
OpenGL. With xvfb installed, scans run on a hidden display.
See README.md for details.
